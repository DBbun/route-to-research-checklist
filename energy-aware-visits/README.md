# Energy-Aware Research Visits

Free tools for study teams, so that people whose energy is the main limit (ME/CFS, Long COVID, persistent symptoms after Lyme, lung and heart disease, cancer fatigue, multiple sclerosis, dialysis and others) can take part in clinical research. Drafts for comment; each file is a tagged PDF with a Word version. Independent work by DBbun LLC, entered in an NIH challenge; not funded or endorsed by NIH. License: CC BY 4.0.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Visit_Protocol_DRAFT_v1` | Energy-Aware Research Visits: a protocol in six parts (designing the study, before, during and after each visit, people who are housebound or bedbound, what to count) |
| `Access_Plan_Template_DRAFT_v1` | One-page Research Visit Access Plan, filled in with each participant at enrollment; asks what the person needs, not their diagnosis |
| `Route_Energy_Cost_2026-11` | Energy cost of the route to three Boston hospitals: distance, standing time, slopes (public elevation map vs walking app), weather and noise, from walks on November 23–24, 2026 |
| `MGH_Arrival_Guide_v1` | Example personal arrival guide for Massachusetts General Hospital: drafted by an AI tool, every fact checked against official pages and on foot |

Evidence on what registered studies ask of participants: see `../participant-burden-screen/`.
