# Trip Experience Survey: a template for study teams

A short, anonymous survey to ask people what makes getting to appointments and research visits hard. It was used in a 2026 pilot in the Boston area, alongside the Route to Research Checklist. Copy it into any form tool, replace the text in [brackets], and remove questions you don't need. Every question is optional.

## Before you share it

- Don't collect names, email addresses or sign-ins. In Google Forms: Settings > Responses > Collect email addresses: Do not collect; Limit to 1 response: off.
- Don't ask for diagnoses or medical details. Ask people not to put names or identifying details in the text boxes.
- Report results only as counts and themes. Quote people only if they said Yes to question 13.
- Test the form with a screen reader before sharing it (for example, Narrator on Windows or VoiceOver on a phone).

## Form description

[Site or study name] wants to make it easier for everyone to get to our visits, including people with disabilities. This 3-minute form is anonymous: please don't include your name or health details. Answers will be summarized only in aggregate. Everyone is welcome to answer, with or without a disability. All questions are optional.

## Questions (all optional)

1. **Which of these describe you?** (Checkboxes)
   - I have a disability
   - I don't have a disability
   - I am a family member or caregiver of someone with a disability
   - Parent or caregiver of a young child
   - I work in healthcare or research
   - Other
   - Prefer not to say

2. **When you travel to appointments, do you use or bring any of these?** (Checkboxes)
   - Manual wheelchair
   - Power wheelchair or scooter
   - Walker or rollator
   - Cane or crutches
   - White cane or guide dog
   - Hearing aids or other hearing support
   - Service animal
   - Someone who helps me
   - Stroller
   - None of these
   - Prefer not to say

3. **If you have a disability, which types?** (Checkboxes)
   - Mobility
   - Blind or low vision
   - Deaf or hard of hearing
   - Chronic illness, fatigue or pain
   - Intellectual or developmental
   - Mental health
   - Speech or communication
   - Temporary injury or condition
   - Other
   - Prefer not to say

4. **Do any of these also make getting to appointments harder for you?** (Checkboxes)
   - Low income or cost of travel
   - No car
   - I can't drive
   - Living outside a city or far from transit
   - Older age
   - English is not my first language
   - Caring for children or others
   - None of these
   - Prefer not to say

5. **How important is each of these to you? (Columns: Essential / Helpful / Not needed / Not sure)** (Grid)
   - Transit stop: elevator at this entrance, and a sign to the accessible entrance
   - Distance and rest: seats along the way, shade or shelter
   - Rest and restrooms: a place to sit or lie down, a restroom nearby
   - Surface: brick, cracks, gaps, tree roots, ice, puddles
   - Crossings: curb ramps, warning strips, signals that beep or talk, enough time to cross
   - Drop-off: loading zone for wheelchair vans, accessible parking near the door
   - Entrance: automatic door, reachable button, signs to the accessible door
   - Inside: distance to the desk, desk height, elevator, restroom, seating
   - Senses: lighting, glare, noise, smoke, smells
   - The day itself: construction detours, weather, ride pickup times
   - Cost: fares, parking and rides paid back
   - Ride booked and paid for by the study
   - Companion: someone can come along, and their travel is covered
   - Remote or home visits: by phone, video or at home when possible

6. **What is missing from this list?** (Paragraph)

7. **Have you ever missed, cancelled or turned down a medical appointment or a research study because getting there was too hard?** (Multiple choice)
   - Yes, a medical appointment
   - Yes, a research study
   - Yes, both
   - No
   - Not sure

8. **If yes, what made it too hard?** (Checkboxes)
   - Transit (no elevator, elevator out of service, no accessible stop)
   - Distance or nowhere to rest
   - Sidewalks, surfaces or curb ramps
   - Drop-off or parking
   - The building entrance or doors
   - Inside the building
   - Weather (snow, ice, heat)
   - Ride timing (paratransit, van or ride pickup)
   - Cost of travel or parking
   - Fatigue or pain: the trip itself was too much
   - No one to go with me
   - Other

9. **What one change would help you most when getting to an appointment?** (Paragraph)

10. **If [site or study name] checked the route to its visits and fixed the problems it found, would you be more likely to take part in a study here?** (Multiple choice)
   - Much more likely
   - Somewhat more likely
   - No difference
   - Not sure

11. **Which state (or country, if outside the US) do you live in?** (Short answer)

12. **How did you hear about this form?** (Multiple choice)
   - [List the places you shared it]
   - Other

13. **May short, anonymous quotes from your written answers be used in reports? Nothing that could identify you will be included.** (Multiple choice)
   - Yes
   - No

## Confirmation message

Thank you. Your answers are anonymous and will only be reported in summary form.
