# Checklist versions

## v3 (2026-11-01)
Two pages instead of one. Most changes came from comments and form responses from people with disabilities and chronic illnesses (see [community-input/THEMES.md](community-input/THEMES.md)).
- **Page 1, the route** (checked on foot): new **Signs** item (a sign to the step-free route where people choose their way; simple words and symbols). Elevators clean, lit and easy to see, and the number of elevators on the whole trip. Seats wherever people wait; restrooms along the way. Stairs, hills, bumpy stretches and railings. Time the walk signal. Drop-off for anyone who asks, placard or not. Crowds and a quiet place to wait. Construction detours step-free and signed; snow and ice. Subtitle now covers the trip by transit, car or ride.
- **Page 2, the visit** (new, for study teams): directions in plain language with photos, timing to the transit timetable, rides booked and paid by the study, cost per visit including lost income, a companion, communication access, rest, fewer trips (remote, home or hybrid visits), and asking every participant what they need.

## v2 (2026-10-28)
- Added **Cost**: "Fare, parking or ride cost? Does the site pay it back?" Form responses named the cost of travel or parking as a reason for missing a research study.
- **Inside** now includes "check-in kiosk usable without sight" (from a community comment).
- Footer notes AI assistance (Claude).

## v1 (2026-10-26)
- First public version: 9 areas (transit stop, distance and rest, surface, crossings, drop-off, entrance, inside, senses, the day itself), built from the first pilot walks.
